# Purchase Order Lead Time Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Purchase Order lead time and supply chain performance.

The project focuses on identifying lead time variations, supplier performance, delays, emergency orders, and the main factors affecting the overall purchasing process.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze Actual Lead Time versus Standard Lead Time
- Monitor On-Time Order Performance
- Identify delayed and emergency orders
- Analyze Lead Time Variance and Delay Days
- Compare supplier performance
- Analyze lead time by country, item type, and cargo port
- Analyze payment terms and their impact on the purchasing process
- Identify areas where lead time can be improved

## 📌 Key KPIs

- Total Purchase Orders
- Total PO Value
- Emergency Orders %
- On-Time Orders %
- Average Actual Lead Time
- Average Standard Lead Time
- Average Lead Time Variance
- Average Delay Days

## 🔍 Analysis Areas

### Lead Time Analysis

- Actual vs. Standard Lead Time
- Monthly Lead Time Trends
- Lead Time by Supplier
- Lead Time by Item Type
- Lead Time by Cargo Port
- Lead Time by Supplier Country

### Supplier Performance

- Average Lead Time by Supplier
- Average PO Value by Supplier
- Supplier Process Analysis
- Supplier Lead Time Variance

### Delay Analysis

- On-Time vs. Delayed Orders
- Average Delay by Month
- Delay by Lead Time Range
- Delay Reasons
- Identification of suppliers and processes contributing to delays

### Purchasing Analysis

- Emergency vs. Normal Orders
- Payment Term Analysis
- PO Value Analysis

## 🔄 Lead Time Process

The overall purchasing lead time was analyzed through the following main stages:

**Confirmation → Readiness → Shipping → Banking → Clearance**

This breakdown helps identify which stage contributes most to the overall lead time.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset used in this project is **synthetic data generated with the assistance of AI for demonstration purposes only**.

It does not represent actual company, supplier, transaction, or confidential business data.

The dataset was designed to simulate realistic Purchase Order and Supply Chain scenarios for analytical and dashboard development purposes.

## 💡 Key Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- How does actual lead time compare with the standard lead time?
- Which suppliers have the highest lead time?
- Which suppliers contribute most to lead time variance?
- Which countries or shipping lanes have longer lead times?
- What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
- How frequently are emergency orders placed?
- Which lead time stage contributes most to the overall cycle time?
- Where are the major delays occurring?
- How does lead time change over time?

## 🚀 Project Purpose

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate how supply chain data can be transformed into interactive dashboards and actionable insights using Power BI.

The project combines **Supply Chain / MRP knowledge with Data Analytics and Business Intelligence** to support better visibility and decision-making.
